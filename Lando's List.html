Lando's List

Preseason Rankings

#1: Devadou (#1 G, #2 A, #1 T, #3 D)

Although he's on a 1 match losing streak, Devan comes in as our preseason #1. He's the favorite for the MVP and Tezos Cup. He looks dialed in since that embarassment vs Jayen and looks like a true force in this league. Many would be shocked if Devan did not walk away from this season with multiple majors, including the coveted green jacket.

#2: Ganesh (#2 G, #1 A, #2 T, #2 D)

Ganesh comes in showing why he can compete for a tour championship. He put up great scores throughout the offseason, came out on top multiple times, and showed aura with his callouts. A lot of people are expecting and rooting for him to overtake Devadou by the end of the year. Not only do people want him to win a green jacket, but they think he has what it takes to win the Tezos Cup.

#3: Amar (#3 G, #3 A, #4 T, #1 D)

Amar was expected to be going through some rookie growing pains during the season. However, he has shown promise in his preseason starts. A -7 in his debut reminded us of a young Tiger Woods. Since then, he has beaten preseason #4 Jayen many times, posted a -12, and beaten the top contender for the Tezos Cup, OUTRIGHT. Big things are in store for Amar this season, including the rookie of the year award.

#4: Jayen (#4 G, #4 A, #3 T, #4 D)

Finally, Jayen comes in at #4 of our rankings. He had a big win in the offsesason over preseason #1 Devadou, but it's what have you done for me lately. This dude has been above par multiple times, he's lost by double digits, he's lost his dignity, and most importantly lost aura. After winning that matchup vs Devadou, he somehow lost money on that same night and was begging for revenge, only to get clapped days later. Now, he's gotten his chain snatched by #3, ROY front runner, Amar. I would be shocked if Jayen won more than 1 tournament this upcoming season.
